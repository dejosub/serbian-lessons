# Lesson 6 teacher guide — How did you spend your day?

## Target

In a three-minute conversation, Amaya can ask and answer where she was and what she did, connect at least four everyday events, and ask one spontaneous follow-up question.

## Sequence — 60 minutes

1. **Cumulative conversation — 6 min:** Greeting, family/pet description, and one meal question.
2. **Today hook — 5 min:** Ask `Како си провела дан?`; accept mixed language and recast two useful answers.
3. **Dialogue — 8 min:** Listen for places and actions, read with Cyrillic leading, switch roles, and personalize.
4. **Question set — 6 min:** Use `где`, `шта`, and `с ким` with true answers and immediate follow-ups.
5. **Action chunks — 7 min:** Establish six female-speaker past chunks through photos or gestures; no full paradigm.
6. **Reading in Latin — 6 min:** Read “An ordinary Tuesday” for sequence and meaning.
7. **Discussion and retelling — 8 min:** Infer why the day was good, retell from prompts, and compare it with Amaya’s day.
8. **Cyrillic second pass — 5 min:** Read the familiar story independently; finish the remaining alphabet review as needed.
9. **Open interview — 7 min:** Three-minute interview each way with mundane details and off-course follow-ups.
10. **Exit performance — 2 min:** Four connected completed actions without notes.

## Boundaries

Treat the past forms as high-value chunks. Correct the helper placement and intelligibility, not every agreement or case form. Latin remains available after the first Cyrillic attempt.

## Session note for Lesson 7

Record which questions and action chunks were spontaneous, whether event order was clear, how much Cyrillic support was needed, and which everyday topics generated conversation.
