# Lesson 3 teacher guide — My family

## Target

The learner can identify and introduce at least three real people using `Ovo je moj/moja ...` and state one true family fact with `Imam` or `Nemam`.

## Before the lesson

- Ask the learner to bring a few family photos or use a hand-drawn family map.
- Confirm the words the family actually uses (`mama/majka`, `tata/otac`, partner labels).
- Record the dialogue naturally and slowly in the selected dialect.
- Show Cyrillic and Latin equally; do not require Cyrillic writing yet.

## Suggested sequence (45–60 minutes)

1. **Cumulative review — 7 min:** Full greeting and `Kako si/ste?` exchange.
2. **Personal hook — 5 min:** Show one photo and say `Ovo je moja/ moj ...`; let meaning emerge.
3. **Dialogue — 10 min:** Listen, sequence four dialogue lines, then read in both scripts.
4. **Vocabulary — 8 min:** Select only the 6–8 family words relevant to the learner; remaining words are recognition items.
5. **`moj/moja` pattern — 8 min:** Sort the selected words into two groups. Avoid a complete gender or case lecture.
6. **`lj/nj` — 5 min:** Model names and short words; point out Cyrillic `љ/њ`.
7. **Exercises — 7 min:** Complete A and B aloud, then check in writing.
8. **Photo role-play — 8 min:** Ask `Ko je ovo?`; learner introduces real people. Reverse roles.
9. **Exit check — 4 min:** Three people plus one `Imam/Nemam` sentence without notes.

## Important grammar note

The dialogue contains `jednog brata` and `jednu sestru`. Treat these as whole useful chunks. Do not teach the accusative paradigm yet. If the number is not needed, `Imam brata/sestru` is enough.

## Answer key

- Practice A: `moj brat`, `moja mama`, `moj dečko`, `moja sestra`, `moj tata`, `moja devojka`.
- Practice B: `Ko je ovo? — Ovo je moj brat, Luka. Imam jednu sestru. Ovo je moja sestra, Ana.`

## Observe for Lesson 4

- Which family words are genuinely useful and should recur?
- Does the learner select `moj/moja` by noun reliably?
- Can she read familiar phrases in Cyrillic without transliteration?
- Does `lj` or `nj` affect intelligibility?
