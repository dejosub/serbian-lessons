Conversation review

Greet me, introduce one person, then offer something and request it with Mogu li da dobijem ...?

Who does what? A small case map

Nominative = the doer or identity
Ana je ovde. · Ovo je Marko.

Accusative = the direct object
Vidim Anu. · Imam jednog brata. · Želim vodu.

For many feminine nouns: -a → -u.

Listen first

Amaya: Ko je ovo?
Marko: Ovo je moja baka. Ona je veoma topla i duhovita.
Amaya: A gde je ona?
Marko: Ona je u Beogradu. Beograd je veliki i živ.

Cyrillic reading strategy

Read a poem in Latin

Ala je lep ovaj svet,
onde potok, ovde cvet;
tamo njiva, ovde sad,
eno sunce, evo hlad.
Tamo Dunav, zlata pun,
onde trava, ovde žbun.
Slavuj peva, ne znam gdi —
ovde srce, ovde ti!

Discuss and react

  • What can the speaker see and hear?
  • Which words mean here and there?
  • What mood does the poem create?
  • What would you change to describe today?
