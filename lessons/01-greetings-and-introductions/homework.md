# Lesson 1 homework

Do one small activity on four days. Stop after about 10 minutes.

## Day 1 — Read and recall

Read the lesson dialogue and “At the door” aloud twice. Cover the English, explain what happens, and answer one discussion question.

## Day 2 — Listen and echo

Listen to the teacher’s natural recording. Pause after each line and imitate its rhythm. Repeat once with the slower recording.

## Day 3 — Write

Complete from memory:

1. ______! Ja sam ______.  
2. Kako se ______?  
3. Drago mi ______.  
4. ______ srpski. Malo!  
5. Vidimo ______!

## Day 4 — Voice note

Record 20–30 seconds:

1. Say hello.
2. Say your name.
3. Ask the listener’s name.
4. Say you are learning Serbian, a little.
5. Say goodbye.

Do not restart for every small mistake. Send one natural attempt.

## Answer key

1. `Zdravo! Ja sam [name].`  
2. `Kako se zoveš?`  
3. `Drago mi je.`  
4. `Učim srpski. Malo!`  
5. `Vidimo se!`
