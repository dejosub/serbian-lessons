# Lesson 4 teacher guide — At the table

## Target

In a two-minute dinner-table role-play, Amaya can offer something, accept or decline, request one item, and give a true reaction while switching appropriately between `ti` and `Vi`.

## Before the lesson

- Choose photos or real examples of foods served by Marko’s family.
- Confirm the family’s most natural wording for offers and requests.
- Prepare a natural and a slow recording of the dialogue and reading.
- Read the latest dated session note and select one Lesson 1–3 phrase and one sound to recycle.

## Sequence — 60 minutes

1. **Cumulative conversation — 6 min:** Greeting, `Kako si/ste?`, and one real-person introduction. Ask a follow-up rather than running isolated recall items.
2. **Meal hook — 5 min:** Reveal a food photo or real item. Elicit reactions and teach only the name needed to discuss it.
3. **Dialogue listening and performance — 8 min:** Listen without text, identify who accepts and declines, then read and switch roles.
4. **Offer-response exchanges — 7 min:** Use true choices with 4–6 relevant foods. Alternate `Hoćeš li` and `Hoćete li` through real social situations.
5. **Reading — 6 min:** Read “Sunday lunch” for gist and details. Avoid line-by-line translation.
6. **Retelling and open discussion — 8 min:** Retell the event, design an ideal meal, and compare family meal habits. Recast useful Serbian without requiring a fully Serbian discussion.
7. **Request and reaction chunks — 6 min:** Model `Mogu li da dobijem ...?`, `Izvolite`, `Volim`, and `Ukusno je` in exchanges. Mention changing food forms only as chunks.
8. **Pronunciation and Cyrillic — 6 min:** Contrast `š/ž`, recycle pure `o`, add the five target letters, and decode the familiar targets.
9. **Communication lab — 6 min:** Run a meal with Marko, then with an older relative. Introduce one unexpected turn: the desired item is missing or the learner is full.
10. **Exit performance — 2 min:** Fresh offer, response, request, and reaction without notes.

## Correction focus

Prioritize the offer form, `molim/hvala`, and intelligible `š/ž`. Give immediate, brief feedback and have Amaya reuse the corrected phrase once in a new exchange.

## Optional repair only

Use the three completion prompts in the handout only if conversation shows that the phrase frame is not available. Do not assign them as the main activity.

## Session note for Lesson 5

Add a dated file in `notes/` recording which food and table phrases were independently retrieved, whether `ti/Vi` survived the role-play, one pronunciation target, which Cyrillic words were decoded, and the topics that produced spontaneous discussion.
