Lesson 2 · Lekcija 2

How are you?

Kako si?

Today: ask, answer, and choose informal ti or polite Vi.
Quick review

Your first conversation

  • Say hello.
  • Introduce yourself.
  • Ask my name.
  • Say “Nice to meet you.”
  • Say you are learning Serbian.
Listen

Two friends

Mila: Zdravo, Emi! Kako si?
Hi, Emmy! How are you?
Emi: Dobro sam, hvala. A ti?
I’m well, thanks. And you?
Mila: Odlično sam!
I’m great!
Listen

A polite exchange

Emi: Dobar dan, gospođo Petrović. Kako ste?
Gđa Petrović: Dobro sam, hvala. A Vi?
Emi: I ja sam dobro.
Notice: ste / Vi replace si / ti.

Conversation pattern

Ask → answer → return

Kako si?
Dobro sam, hvala. A ti?

Change both the person and the answer.

Role-play

Three situations

  1. A close friend arrives.
  2. You meet an older relative for the first time.
  3. You greet two cousins together.
Choose si/ti or ste/Vi before you begin.
Reading

A quick call

Mila zove Emi. Emi je kod kuće.
„Zdravo! Kako si?” pita Mila.
„Umorna sam, ali dobro sam,” kaže Emi.
Mila kaže: „I ja sam umorna!”

zove = calls · ali = but · pita = asks

Discussion

Talk about the reading

  • Who calls whom?
  • How does Emi feel? How does Mila feel?
  • Why might they be tired?
  • What would your true answer be today?
Use Serbian where you can; use English where you need it. Then retell the call.
Exit check

Try without notes

  • Greet me.
  • Ask how I am.
  • Answer my question.
  • Return it correctly.
  • Say goodbye.
Odlično! You can now extend your Serbian greeting.
