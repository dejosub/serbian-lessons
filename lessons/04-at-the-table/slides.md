# Serbian 4
## At the table · Za stolom

Offer, accept, decline, and ask for food or drink.

---

# Conversation review

Greet me, ask how I am, introduce one person, and say one true family fact.

---

# Listen first

**Baka:** Amaya, hoćeš li pitu?<br>
**Amaya:** Da, molim. Hvala!<br>
**Baka:** A Marko, hoćeš li još?<br>
**Marko:** Ne, hvala. Sit sam.<br>
**Amaya:** Voda, molim.

Who accepts? Who declines? What does Amaya ask for?

---

# Four useful moves

**Hoćeš li ...?** — Would you like ...? (`ti`)<br>
**Hoćete li ...?** — Would you like ...? (`Vi`)<br>
**Da, molim.** — Yes, please.<br>
**Ne, hvala.** — No, thank you.

---

# Food and drink

`pita` pie · `hleb` bread · `sir` cheese<br>
`salata` salad · `voda` water · `kafa` coffee

Choose the items that belong at a real family meal.

---

# Ask for something

**Voda, molim.** — Water, please.<br>
**Mogu li da dobijem vodu?** — May I have some water?<br>
**Izvolite.** — Here you are. / Please.

Learn the whole phrases; no case table today.

---

# A true reaction

**Volim pitu.** — I like pie.<br>
**Ne volim kafu.** — I don’t like coffee.<br>
**Ukusno je.** — It is delicious.<br>
**Sita sam. / Sit sam.** — I am full.

---

# `š` and `ž`

**š**: `šta`, `još`<br>
**ž**: `želim`, `kaže`

Keep the vowel in `još` short and steady.

---

# Cyrillic bridge

`Ш = Š` · `Ж = Ž` · `З = Z` · `Г = G` · `Ф = F`

**још** · **жена** · **сир** · **вода** · **хлеб**

Read for sound first; meaning is already known.

---

# Read: Sunday lunch

> Amaya i Marko su kod bake i deke. Na stolu su pita, salata, sir i hleb. Baka pita: „Amaya, hoćeš li još pite?” Amaya kaže: „Da, molim. Pita je veoma ukusna!”

`baka` grandmother · `deka` grandfather · `na stolu` on the table · `veoma` very

---

# Talk about it

- Where are Amaya and Marko?
- What is on the table?
- What does Amaya accept?
- What would be on the table at your ideal Serbian meal?

Retell, react, and ask one follow-up question.

---

# Dinner-table role-play

Round 1: Marko offers food.<br>
Round 2: an older relative offers food.<br>
Round 3: ask for something that is across the table.

Use a true preference at least once.

---

# Exit conversation

Without notes: offer one item, accept or decline, ask for a drink, and react to the food.
