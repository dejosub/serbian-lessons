# Lesson 11 teacher guide — Colors

## Target

Amaya can describe the colors of clothes, food, and places with correct masculine, feminine, and neuter endings in common phrases; complete a polite clothes-shop exchange; and say what people are wearing in a family photo.

## Before the lesson

- Gather eight to ten colored objects you can touch: clothes, fruit, a mug, a book, a pen.
- Find one autumn photo of Kalemegdan or the Danube in Belgrade.
- Choose one real family photo with visible clothing.
- For the shop scenario, prepare one problem: the jacket is not available in her color, it is too expensive, or it comes only in dark blue.
- Lesson 10 reached its grammar slide (slide 6 of 14) and stopped there; the accusative slide went very well. The plan phrases, months, Baka’s message, and the scenarios were never practiced in class. This lesson opens with the plan phrases as a self-contained warm-up; Baka’s message is in the homework, and months and birthdays move to Lesson 13, where `у августу` is the locative.
- Bring the two Lesson 10 “busy week” cards, or make them now: four or five commitments each, with only one or two free slots in common.
- Amaya enjoyed the word roots of the days of the week. Read the Word origins slide beforehand and let her guess each origin before you give it.

## Sequence — 60 minutes

1. **Warm-up: make a plan — 7 min:** Read the six phrases once, then plan with the busy-week cards: a walk, a concert, lunch with Baka and Deda. Refuse her first suggestion (`Не могу у суботу, радим.`) and wait for a counterproposal. Listen for `у` + day in the accusative from last week.
2. **Color hook — 3 min:** Touch objects and ask `Које је боје?`; Amaya repeats, then asks you.
3. **Colors — 4 min:** Go through the swatches, then `светло-` and `тамно-`.
4. **Word origins — 4 min:** Before each arrow, ask her to guess: what could `црвен` have to do with `црв`? What does `-аст` add? Finish with `ружичаст`. If she asks for more, `зелен` is related to `зеље` (greens) and `жут` to `злато` (gold) in older Slavic; keep it to one or two extras.
5. **Grammar slide: gender and agreement — 12 min:** Name `род` and `слагање`, then work through the example bank one noun at a time: she gives each real object a color with the right ending (`-`, `-а`, `-о`). Finish with the accusative row: the jacket is what the wearing happens to, so it is the object, as `кафу` was in Lesson 10. Add the row to the grammar map. Mention `плави џемпер` once; do not drill definite forms.
6. **Vowel-less `р` — 2 min:** `црн, црвен, Србија, српски, четрдесет`; say `цр-вен` without an inserted vowel.
7. **Dialogue and role switch — 4 min:** Listen with print hidden: what is she wearing in the end? Switch roles and choose different clothes.
8. **Clothes and wearing — 3 min:** `Шта носиш данас? — Носим ...` about both of you; notice `плаву јакну`.
9. **Reading in Cyrillic — 6 min:** Latin help covers the long words. Read once for the place and season, once for the colors.
10. **Discussion — 4 min:** Look at the photo together and decide what color the Danube is.
11. **Scenarios — 8 min:** Clothes shop with one problem; the family photo if time remains. What am I thinking of? is in the handout and homework, and opens Lesson 12.
12. **Exit — 3 min**

## Grammar example bank — gender and agreement

- masculine, no ending: `џемпер`, `сир`, `хлеб`, `пасуљ`, `парк`, `пас`, `чај`, `Дунав`
- feminine, `-а`: `јакна`, `кафа`, `вода`, `пита`, `сарма`, `јабука`, `мачка`, `кућа`
- neuter, `-о` or `-е`: `небо`, `место`, `дрвеће`
- accusative, only feminine changes (for things): `Носим плаву јакну.` `Имам белу мачку.` `Волим зелени чај.`

## Push moves

- In the shop: `Немамо плаву, али имамо тамноплаву.` or `Ова кошта десет хиљада динара.` Wait for her next question.
- In the guessing game, answer only `Да` or `Не` and let silence prompt another question.
- During the photo description, ask `А ко носи црвено?` to make her look again.

## Boundaries

Colors recycle adjective agreement from Lesson 5 and add the neuter `-о`. Correct agreement in the phrases she uses most (`плава јакна`, `плаво небо`); let other slips go. `у плавој боји` is a ready-made chunk, not a lesson on the locative.

## Script and pronunciation

Primarily Cyrillic; every new color, clothing word, and question has its Latin beside it on the slide, and long words in the reading are in Latin at the bottom. The vowel-less `р` is the pronunciation focus: say `црвен` as two syllables, `цр-вен`, without an inserted vowel.

## Session note for Lesson 12

Record how far you got and how long the grammar slide took, whether she proposed and declined plans without the slide, how agreement held up in fast speech, how she handled the shop problem, how many questions she needed in the guessing game, and whether the school or location topic should come next.
