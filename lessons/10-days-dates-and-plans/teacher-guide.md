# Lesson 10 teacher guide — Days, months, and plans

## Target

In a two-minute planning conversation, Amaya can ask about someone’s schedule, propose a plan with a day and time, decline one option, and agree on another. She can understand a short family message and answer it aloud or in writing.

## Before the lesson

- Make two “busy week” cards, one for each of you, each with four to five commitments on different days and times (for example: `у понедељак ради`, `у среду увече бас`, `у суботу ујутру пијаца`). Leave only one or two free slots in common.
- Find out the family’s slava (saint and date) and how Baka and Deda usually celebrate it.
- Decide how Baka would address Amaya in a message; replace `Драга Амаја` with `Драга Амајо` if that is what she would write.
- Read the Lesson 9 session note and choose numbers to recycle as times.

## Sequence — 60 minutes

1. **Cumulative conversation — 6 min:** Prices, ages, and feelings; push with `Зашто?`
2. **The week — 5 min:** Say the days with a calendar in front of you; ask `Који је данас дан?` and `Шта радиш у ...?`
3. **On Saturday — 4 min:** `у суботу / у среду / у недељу`; point out the `-а → -у` change once.
4. **Yesterday, today, tomorrow — 4 min:** True sentences with `јуче`, `сутра`, `поподне`, `увече`, `следеће недеље`.
5. **Sound sort — 3 min:** `четвртак, ручак, Божић, рођендан, хоћеш, џез концерт`; Amaya points, then reads and you point.
6. **Dialogue and plan phrases — 7 min:** Listen with print hidden; then role-switch and change the event, day, and time.
7. **Months and birthdays — 4 min:** `Када ти је рођендан? — У ...` for everyone in the family.
8. **Reading Baka’s message — 6 min:** Latin help covers the long words. Read once for the event, once for the options.
9. **Discussion and reply — 6 min:** Answer Baka aloud; notice `бако` and `до петка` briefly.
10. **Family holidays — 3 min:** Tell the real story of your slava in simple Serbian.
11. **Scenarios — 10 min:** Find a time (busy-week cards) and Call Baka.
12. **Exit — 2 min**

## Push moves

- In Find a time, refuse her first suggestion (`Не могу у суботу, радим.`) and wait for a counterproposal.
- As Baka on the phone, change the time (`Не у један—у два!`) and add a request (`Донесите хлеб!`) to see whether she confirms.
- Ask `Шта да донесемо?` back to her and let her decide.

## Boundaries

`у` + day and `у` + month are chunks. Name the accusative (days) and locative (months) only if she asks; do not tabulate. Accept `у субота` once, then recast. The vocative `бако` is for recognition and one useful phrase, not a new topic.

## Script and pronunciation

Primarily Cyrillic, with Latin for days, months, and long words in the reading. Model question rhythm on `Када?`, `У колико сати?`, and `Хоћеш ли да идемо?`; let the voice rise on the important word. Keep the hard/soft check short—it is maintenance now.

## Session note for Lesson 11

Record which days and months she produces without hesitation, whether she declines and counterproposes independently, how she handled Baka’s message, and what she wants to know about family holidays.
