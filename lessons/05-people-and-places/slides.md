# Serbian 5
## Људи, љубимци и места
### Ljudi, ljubimci i mesta

Describe someone or somewhere in connected sentences.

---

# Conversation review

Greet me, introduce one person, then offer something and request it with **Mogu li da dobijem ...?**

---

# Who does what? A small case map

**Nominative = who is there or being identified**<br>
`Ovo je Ana.` · `Ana je visoka.`

**Accusative = the direct object after an action**<br>
`Vidim Anu.` · `Opisujem Anu.`

What changed? For many feminine nouns, **-a → -u**: `Ana → Anu`, `sestra → sestru`, `voda → vodu`.

---

# Listen first

**Amaya:** Ko je ovo?<br>
**Marko:** Ovo je moja baka. Ona je veoma vesela i duhovita.<br>
**Amaya:** A gde je ona?<br>
**Marko:** Ona je u Beogradu. Beograd je veliki i živ.

Who is described? Where is she?

---

# Describe a person

**On je ...** — He is ...<br>
**Ona je ...** — She is ...<br>

`visok/visoka` tall · `mlad/mlada` young<br>
`veseo/vesela` cheerful · `duhovit/duhovita` funny<br>
`brz/brza` fast

Choose words that fit a real person.

**љубимац** · `ljubimac` — pet<br>
**пас** · `pas` — dog · **мачка** · `mačka` — cat

---

# Describe a place

**Gde je ...?** — Where is ...?<br>
**On/Ona je u ...** — He/She is in ...<br>
**Ovde je ...** — Here it is ...<br>

`veliki/velika` big · `mali/mala` small<br>
`lep/lepa` beautiful · `živ/živa` lively

---

# Notice the pattern

| with `on` | with `ona` |
|---|---|
| visok | visoka |
| duhovit | duhovita |
| lep | lepa |

Use the phrase; explain the pattern after it is meaningful.

---

# Add detail

**i** — and<br>
**ali** — but<br>
**veoma** — very<br>

> Ona je vesela i duhovita.<br>
> Grad je veliki, ali lep.

# Cyrillic reading strategy

Read a whole familiar phrase. Mark only the letter that blocks you. Check Latin after a full attempt.

**Она је у Београду.**<br>
**Град је велики и жив.**

---

# The final five Cyrillic letters

`Ч = Č` · `Ћ = Ć` · `Џ = DŽ` · `Ђ = Đ` · `У = U`

**кућа** · **дечко** · **Ђорђе** · **џем**

The sounds and words are familiar; these Cyrillic symbols are new. With this set, all 30 letters have now been introduced. Hear and say each word before decoding it.

# Read a poem in Latin

> Ala je lep ovaj svet,<br>
> onde potok, ovde cvet;<br>
> tamo njiva, ovde sad,<br>
> eno sunce, evo hlad.<br>
> Tamo Dunav, zlata pun,<br>
> onde trava, ovde žbun.<br>
> Slavuj peva, ne znam gdi —<br>
> ovde srce, ovde ti!

**Key words:** `potok` stream · `cvet` flower · `njiva` field · `hlad` shade<br>
`žbun` bush · `slavuj` nightingale · `gdi` old poetic **gde**

---

# Discuss and react

- What can the speaker see and hear?
- Which words mean here and there?
- What mood does the poem create?
- What would you change to describe today?

# Read again in Cyrillic

> Ала је леп овај свет,<br>
> онде поток, овде цвет;<br>
> тамо њива, овде сад,<br>
> ено сунце, ево хлад.<br>
> Тамо Дунав, злата пун,<br>
> онде трава, овде жбун.<br>
> Славуј пева, не знам гди —<br>
> овде срце, овде ти!

**Кључне речи:** `поток` stream · `цвет` flower · `њива` field · `хлад` shade<br>
`жбун` bush · `славуј` nightingale · `гди` old poetic **где**

# From the poem to your world

What would your version of **ovaj svet** include? Choose a real photo with one meaningful person or pet in a place that matters. Say:

1. who is there;
2. where the person is;
3. two descriptions;
4. why this person, pet, or place belongs in your world.

Ask your partner a follow-up question.

---

# Exit performance

Without notes, describe one person or pet in a meaningful place in at least four connected Serbian sentences.
