# Lesson 7 — Шта волиш? · Likes, interests, and pets

## Today I can ...

- say what I like, dislike, and enjoy
- ask about another person’s preferences
- discuss music, food, family activities, and pets
- explain one preference with a simple reason

## Dialogue

**Амаја:** Шта волиш да радиш увече? · `Šta voliš da radiš uveče?`  
**Марко:** Волим да слушам музику и да шетам пса. А ти?  
**Амаја:** Волим да свирам бас. Свиђа ми се и српска храна, али не волим кафу.

## Core language

`волим · volim` — I like/love · `не волим · ne volim` — I do not like · `свиђа ми се · sviđa mi se` — I like it/it appeals to me · `омиљен/омиљена · omiljen/omiljena` — favorite

Questions: `Шта волиш?` — What do you like? · `Шта волиш да радиш?` — What do you like doing? · `Која ти је омиљена музика/храна?` — What is your favorite music/food?

## Add a reason

`зато што · zato što` — because · `занимљиво · zanimljivo` — interesting · `укусно · ukusno` — delicious · `забавно · zabavno` — fun · `опушта ме · opušta me` — it relaxes me

## Reading — Everyone likes something different

> У Марковој породици свако воли нешто друго. Бака воли да кува, а деда воли да чита новине. Марко воли музику и дуге шетње са псом. Амаја воли да свира бас и да разговара са породицом. Не воле увек исту храну, али воле да вечерају заједно.

*In Marko’s family everyone likes something different. Grandma likes cooking, and Grandpa likes reading the newspaper. Marko likes music and long walks with the dog. Amaya likes playing bass and talking with the family. They do not always like the same food, but they like eating dinner together.*

### Discuss

Who likes what? Which preferences seem connected? What does your family enjoy together? Is a shared activity more important than sharing the same taste?

## Preference map

Choose four real people or pets. Say one preference for each, then ask your partner to challenge or extend one claim. Use a reason where possible.

## Open conversation

Choose two: music, bass, Serbian food, household activities, pets, family traditions, or places. Speak for two minutes, then switch topic after an unexpected follow-up.
