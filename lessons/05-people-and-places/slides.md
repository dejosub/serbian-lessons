# Serbian 5
## People and places · Ljudi i mesta

Describe someone or somewhere in connected sentences.

---

# Conversation review

Greet me, introduce one person, then offer and request something at the table.

---

# Listen first

**Amaya:** Ko je ovo?<br>
**Marko:** Ovo je moja baka. Ona je veoma topla i duhovita.<br>
**Amaya:** A gde je ona?<br>
**Marko:** Ona je u Beogradu. Beograd je veliki i živ.

Who is described? Where is she?

---

# Describe a person

**On je ...** — He is ...<br>
**Ona je ...** — She is ...<br>

`visok/visoka` tall · `mlad/mlada` young<br>
`topao/topla` warm · `duhovit/duhovita` funny

Choose words that fit a real person.

---

# Describe a place

**Gde je ...?** — Where is ...?<br>
**On/Ona je u ...** — He/She is in ...<br>
**Ovde je ...** — Here it is ...<br>

`veliki/velika` big · `mali/mala` small<br>
`lep/lepa` beautiful · `živ/živa` lively

---

# Notice the pattern

| with `on` | with `ona` |
|---|---|
| visok | visoka |
| duhovit | duhovita |
| lep | lepa |

Use the phrase; explain the pattern after it is meaningful.

---

# Add detail

**i** — and<br>
**ali** — but<br>
**veoma** — very<br>

> Ona je topla i duhovita.<br>
> Grad je veliki, ali lep.

---

# Cyrillic reading strategy

Read a whole familiar phrase. Mark only the letter that blocks you. Check Latin after a full attempt.

**Она је у Београду.**<br>
**Град је велики и жив.**

---

# New letters

`Ч = Č` · `Ћ = Ć` · `Џ = DŽ` · `Ђ = Đ` · `У = U`

**кућа** · **дечко** · **Ђорђе** · **џем**

Hear and say the word before decoding it.

---

# Read: At Kalemegdan

> Mila i njen deda su na Kalemegdanu. Deda je miran i duhovit. Park je velik i lep. Beograd je danas veoma živ. Mila voli ovo mesto.

`deda` grandfather · `miran` calm · `park` park · `mesto` place

---

# Discuss and retell

- Who is with Mila?
- How is her grandfather described?
- What is the place like?
- Which person and place would you connect in your own story?

---

# Photo conversation

Choose a real photo. Say:

1. who is there;
2. where the person is;
3. two descriptions;
4. why the person or place matters.

Ask your partner a follow-up question.

---

# Exit performance

Without notes, describe one person and one place in at least four connected Serbian sentences.
