# Serbian 2
## How are you? · Kako si?

Ask, answer, and choose informal `ti` or polite `Vi`.

---

# Quick review

Say hello, introduce yourself, ask my name, say “Nice to meet you,” and say you are learning Serbian.

---

# Listen: friends

**Mila:** Zdravo, Emi! Kako si?  
**Emi:** Dobro sam, hvala. A ti?  
**Mila:** Odlično sam!

---

# Listen: polite

**Emi:** Dobar dan, gospođo Petrović. Kako ste?  
**Gospođa Petrović:** Dobro sam, hvala. A Vi?  
**Emi:** I ja sam dobro.

---

# Choose an answer

**Odlično sam.** — I’m great.  
**Dobro sam.** — I’m well.  
**Nije loše.** — Not bad.  
**Umorna / Umoran sam.** — I’m tired.

---

# `ti` or `Vi`?

| Informal | Polite or plural |
|---|---|
| Kako **si**? | Kako **ste**? |
| A **ti**? | A **Vi**? |

---

# `č` and `ć`

**č** — firmer: `čaj`, `odlično`  
**ć** — softer: `ćao`, `kuća`

Listen. Imitate. Keep talking.

---

# `dž` and `đ`

**dž** — firmer: `džem`  
**đ** — softer: `gospođa`, `Đorđe`

---

# Cyrillic bridge

You already know the sounds and words. Now meet:

`К = K` · `С = S` · `И = I` · `Т = T`<br>
`Х = H` · `В = V` · `Л = L`

Notice: Cyrillic `С` sounds like Latin `S`, and `В` sounds like Latin `V`.

---

# Decode familiar language

**Како си?** · `Kako si?`<br>
**А ти?** · `A ti?`<br>
**Хвала.** · `Hvala.`

Point to each letter as you read. Meaning first; speed later.

---

# Conversation pattern

> Kako si?  
> Dobro sam, hvala. A ti?

Change the person and the answer.

---

# Role-play

1. A close friend arrives.
2. You meet an older relative for the first time.
3. You greet two cousins together.

---

# Read: A quick call

> Mila zove Emi. Emi je kod kuće.<br>
> „Zdravo! Kako si?” pita Mila.<br>
> „Umorna sam, ali dobro sam,” kaže Emi.<br>
> Mila kaže: „I ja sam umorna!”

**zove** = calls · **ali** = but · **pita** = asks

---

# Talk about the reading

- Who calls whom?
- How does Emi feel? How does Mila feel?
- Why might they be tired?
- What would your true answer be today?

Use Serbian where you can; use English where you need it.

---

# Exit check

Without notes: greet me, ask how I am, answer my question, return it correctly, and say goodbye.
