# Lesson 8 homework — Домаћи задатак

Choose three activities, about ten minutes each.

1. **Read and retell:** Read the Amaya passage in Cyrillic, identify the emotional change and cause, then retell it without looking.
2. **Three-moment voice note:** Describe how you felt this morning, during the day, and now. Use two intensities and one `јер` reason.
3. **Response practice:** Choose three fictional situations and respond with `Баш ми је драго`, `Жао ми је`, `Разумем`, or `Хоћеш ли да причамо?` Explain why the response fits.
4. **Conversation with Marko:** Ask `Како се осећаш данас?` Follow with `Зашто?` and one question based on his answer. Respond before sharing your own feeling. Ask him which Serbian response sounds most natural in his family.
5. **Listen and point:** Ask Marko to mix `осећам`, `срећна`, `узбуђена`, and `свиђа` without showing them. Identify `ћ` or `ђ`, then repeat the complete word naturally.

## Self-check

Translate: “I’m a little worried, but I’m happy because we are together.”
