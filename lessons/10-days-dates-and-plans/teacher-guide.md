# Lesson 10 teacher guide — Days, months, and plans

## Target

In a two-minute planning conversation, Amaya can ask about someone’s schedule, propose a plan with a day and time, decline one option, and agree on another. She can understand a short family message and answer it aloud or in writing.

## Before the lesson

- Make two “busy week” cards, one for each of you, each with four to five commitments on different days and times (for example: `у понедељак ради`, `у среду увече бас`, `у суботу ујутру пијаца`). Leave only one or two free slots in common.
- Find out the family’s slava (saint and date) and how Baka and Deda usually celebrate it; Family holidays is now in the handout and homework.
- Decide how Baka would address Amaya in a message; replace `Драга Амаја` with `Драга Амајо` if that is what she would write.
- Lesson 9 ended on its grammar slide (slide 9 of 14) after a long, enjoyable session working through nouns in the genitive. Don’t assume she remembers it or read its text at home: the market stall opens this lesson as a self-contained warm-up, and the New friend scenario moves to homework.
- Read the rhyme aloud a few times beforehand so you can model its rhythm.

## Sequence — 60 minutes

1. **Warm-up: market stall — 8 min:** Play the seller; she has `1.000 динара` for Sunday lunch and the phrases on the slide. Push back on one price and get one total wrong. Listen for the genitive after numbers (`два кила`, `пет јабука`).
2. **The week — 3 min:** Say the days with a calendar in front of you; ask `Који је данас дан?` and `Шта радиш у ...?`
3. **Игра коло — 5 min:** Play the recording (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPcCZ5-8doI) once, then read the rhyme aloud together, once slowly and once in rhythm. She finds each day and says what it does; ask the two questions on the slide. The rhyme makes the days boys and girls—point out `он` and `она` as a preview of Lesson 11. `коловођа` is the first dancer, who leads; the one `на кецу` is the last. Explain only the words she asks about; no memorizing.
4. **When? — 3 min:** True sentences with `јуче`, `сутра`, `поподне`, `увече`, and `викенд`.
5. **Grammar slide: the accusative — 10 min:** Start from its role, not its question: the subject does the action; the accusative is the person or thing the action happens to. Point at the parts of `Амаја воли кафу`. Then name it from what she already says: `Волим кафу`, `једну сестру`, `у суботу`, `Дајте ми ...`. Then work through the example bank one noun at a time, as with the genitive in Lesson 9: she makes a true sentence with `Волим ...`, `Имам ...`, `Дајте ми ...`, or `У ...` + day. Finish with the plural column: only masculine changes (`ћевапи → ћевапе`, `пси → псе`); feminine and neuter look like the dictionary plural. Add the row to the grammar map.
6. **Dialogue — 4 min:** Listen with print hidden; then role-switch and change the event, day, and time.
7. **Make a plan — 4 min:** Propose, accept, decline, counterpropose.
8. **Months and birthdays — 4 min:** `Када ти је рођендан? — У ...` for everyone in the family.
9. **Reading Baka’s message — 6 min:** Latin help covers the long words. Read once for the event, once for the options.
10. **Discussion — 4 min**
11. **Scenarios — 7 min:** Find a time (busy-week cards); Call Baka with the `бако` reply if time remains, otherwise as homework with Marko.
12. **Exit — 2 min**

## Grammar example bank — accusative

- feminine `-а → -у`: `кафа`, `вода`, `сестра`, `бака`, `сарма`, `пита`, `јабука`, `мачка`, `кућа`, `субота`, `среда`, `недеља`
- masculine things, no change: `сир`, `хлеб`, `пасуљ`, `парк`, `понедељак`, `петак`
- masculine people and animals, add `-а`: `брат → брата`; `пас → пса` loses its `а`, like `љубимац → љубимца`
- watch: `деда → деду` changes like `бака`, because it ends in `-а`
- neuter, no change: `место`, `учење`
- plural: `јабуке → јабуке`, `мачке → мачке`, `ћевапи → ћевапе`, `пси → псе`, `места → места`

## Push moves

- In Find a time, refuse her first suggestion (`Не могу у суботу, радим.`) and wait for a counterproposal.
- As Baka on the phone, change the time (`Не у један—у два!`) and add a request (`Донесите хлеб!`) to see whether she confirms.
- Ask `Шта да донесемо?` back to her and let her decide.

## Boundaries

The accusative is the grammar focus: name it from its role, singular first, then the short plural column. `у` + month stays a chunk; call it the locative only as a preview of Lesson 13. Accept `у субота` once, then recast. The vocative `бако` is for recognition and one useful phrase, not a new topic.

## Script and pronunciation

Primarily Cyrillic; every day, month, and plan phrase has its Latin next to it on the slide, and long words in the reading are in Latin at the bottom. Model question rhythm on `Када?`, `У колико сати?`, and `Хоћеш ли да идемо?`; let the voice rise on the important word.

## Session note for Lesson 11

Record how far you got and how long the grammar slide took, which days and months she produces without hesitation, whether she declines and counterproposes independently, how she handled Baka’s message, and what she wants to know about family holidays.
