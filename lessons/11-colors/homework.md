# Lesson 11 homework — Домаћи задатак

Choose three activities, about ten minutes each.

1. **Read and retell:** Read “The colors of Kalemegdan” in Cyrillic, using the Latin help. Retell the walk in colors without looking.
2. **What I’m wearing:** Each morning this week, say what you are wearing: `Данас носим ...` Add one color you like and one you don’t: `Више волим ... него ...`
3. **Color walk voice note:** Record 60–90 seconds describing five things you see outside—the sky, trees, cars, buildings, people’s clothes.
4. **Conversation with Marko:** Play What am I thinking of? Marko thinks of something; you ask yes/no questions (`Да ли је велико?`, `Да ли се једе?`, `Које је боје?`) and find it in ten. Then switch. Finish by asking `Која ти је омиљена боја? Зашто?`
5. **Shop role-play with Marko:** Marko is the seller. Ask for a jacket in your color, try it on, and ask the price. Marko says one problem; solve it in Serbian.

## Self-check

Translate: “I’m wearing a blue T-shirt and a black jacket. The sky is light blue.”

Answer: `Носим плаву мајицу и црну јакну. Небо је светлоплаво.` · `Nosim plavu majicu i crnu jaknu. Nebo je svetloplavo.`

Translate: “Do you have this jacket in green? Can I try it on?”

Answer: `Имате ли ову јакну у зеленој боји? Могу ли да пробам?` · `Imate li ovu jaknu u zelenoj boji? Mogu li da probam?`
