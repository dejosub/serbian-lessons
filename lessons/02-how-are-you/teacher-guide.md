# Lesson 2 teacher guide — How are you?

## Target

The learner can conduct a short greeting, ask and answer “How are you?”, and switch appropriately between `ti` and `Vi`.

## Before the lesson

- Record both dialogues naturally and slowly.
- Prepare photos or names of one friend, one relative, and one unfamiliar adult for `ti/Vi` choices.
- Review the Lesson 1 progress entry and recycle any weak phrase.

## Suggested sequence (45–60 minutes)

1. **Unscripted review — 7 min:** Perform the Lesson 1 exchange. Correct only one previously selected target.
2. **Dialogue — 10 min:** Listen without text, identify mood, then follow the text and switch roles.
3. **Answers — 8 min:** Place response cards on a scale from tired to excellent. Ask rapidly; learner chooses a true answer.
4. **`ti/Vi` — 8 min:** Sort real people and situations. Explain that family habits vary; follow this family’s usage.
5. **Pronunciation — 8 min:** Contrast `č/ć`, then `dž/đ`. Model whole words, not isolated sounds only. Accept comprehensible approximations.
6. **Cyrillic bridge — 7 min:** After the phrases are understood in speech and Latin, introduce `К С И Т Х В Л`. Decode only `Како си?`, `А ти?`, and `Хвала`; point out the potentially misleading `С` and `В`. The Cyrillic spellings of the pronunciation pairs are recognition-only previews.
7. **Selective practice — 5 min:** Use A and B only where the learner needs support; skip repetitive items when she answers reliably.
8. **Reading and discussion — 10 min:** Read “A quick call” in Latin, establish who calls whom and how both people feel, then discuss possible reasons and the learner’s real answer today. Accept English or mixed-language ideas and recast useful Serbian naturally. End with a brief retelling.
9. **Role-play — 8 min:** Friend at home; older relative at a gathering; two relatives together.
10. **Exit check — 4 min:** Use a new person/situation without notes and update `progress.md`.

## Cyrillic boundary

Assess recognition of the seven target letters only in familiar words. Do not ask the learner to read the dialogues or mini-reading in Cyrillic; they are Latin-led language work.

## Correction focus

Prioritize `Kako si/ste?`, the matching `A ti/Vi?`, and a clear distinction between the two question forms. Do not correct every pronunciation contrast during role-play.

## Answer key

- Practice A: 1 `si`, 2 `ste`, 3 `ste`, 4 `si`.
- Practice B: `Dobro sam, hvala. A ti?`; `Kako ste? — Odlično sam!`

## Observe for Lesson 3

- Does the learner retrieve an answer without translating?
- Is `Vi` socially useful in her likely family situations?
- Which of `č/ć` or `dž/đ` needs recycling?
- Collect names and relationships for the personalized family lesson.
