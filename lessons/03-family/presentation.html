Family interview

Ask, answer, follow up

  • Ко је ово? · Ko je ovo?
  • Имаш ли брата или сестру? · Imaš li brata ili sestru?
  • Где је он / она? · Gde je on / ona?
Follow one interesting answer. Ask for a word when you need one.
Reading

An old photo

Emi gleda staru fotografiju kod kuće.
Na fotografiji su njena mama, njen brat Luka i njena baka.
Mama je pored bake.
Luka je sada u Beogradu, ali baka živi u Nišu.
Emi često zove baku: „Zdravo, bako! Kako si?”

pored = next to · živi = lives · često = often

Discussion

Understand, infer, connect

  • Which three people are in the photo?
  • Who is next to Grandma?
  • Where do Luka and Grandma live?
  • Why might Emi call Grandma often?
  • What family photo would you choose?
Cyrillic second pass
