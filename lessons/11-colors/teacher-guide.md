# Lesson 11 teacher guide — Colors

## Target

Amaya can describe the colors of clothes, food, and places with correct masculine, feminine, and neuter endings in common phrases; complete a polite clothes-shop exchange; and identify a hidden object by asking yes/no questions.

## Before the lesson

- Gather eight to ten colored objects you can touch: clothes, fruit, a mug, a book, a pen.
- Find one autumn photo of Kalemegdan or the Danube in Belgrade.
- Choose one real family photo with visible clothing.
- For the shop scenario, prepare one problem: the jacket is not available in her color, it is too expensive, or it comes only in dark blue.
- Read the Lesson 10 session note and choose two plan phrases and one price to recycle.

## Sequence — 60 minutes

1. **Cumulative conversation — 6 min:** Weekend plans, times, and prices; push with `А шта још?`
2. **Color hook — 5 min:** Touch objects and ask `Које је боје?`; Amaya repeats, then asks you.
3. **Colors and three endings — 7 min:** Go through the swatches; then sort real objects into `-`, `-а`, `-о`. Mention `плави џемпер` once; do not drill definite forms.
4. **Vowel-less `р` and sound check — 5 min:** `црн, црвен, Србија, српски, четрдесет`; then single words for hard/soft (`наранџаст, смеђ, одећа, џеп, чарапе, Божић`), print hidden first. No sentences or tongue twisters.
5. **Dialogue and role switch — 5 min:** Listen with print hidden: what is she wearing in the end? Switch roles and choose different clothes.
6. **Clothes and wearing — 5 min:** `Шта носиш данас? — Носим ...` about both of you; notice `плаву јакну`.
7. **Reading in Cyrillic — 6 min:** Latin help covers the long words. Read once for the place and season, once for the colors.
8. **Discussion — 6 min:** Look at the photo together and decide what color the Danube is.
9. **Scenarios — 12 min:** Clothes shop with one problem; then What am I thinking of? with both roles.
10. **Exit — 3 min**

## Push moves

- In the shop: `Немамо плаву, али имамо тамноплаву.` or `Ова кошта десет хиљада динара.` Wait for her next question.
- In the guessing game, answer only `Да` or `Не` and let silence prompt another question.
- During the photo description, ask `А ко носи црвено?` to make her look again.

## Boundaries

Colors recycle adjective agreement from Lesson 5 and add the neuter `-о`. Correct agreement in the phrases she uses most (`плава јакна`, `плаво небо`); let other slips go. `у плавој боји` is a ready-made chunk, not a lesson on the locative.

## Script and pronunciation

Primarily Cyrillic; every new color, clothing word, and question has its Latin beside it on the slide, and long words in the reading are in Latin at the bottom. The vowel-less `р` is the pronunciation focus: say `црвен` as two syllables, `цр-вен`, without an inserted vowel.

## Session note for Lesson 12

Record which colors she used independently, how agreement held up in fast speech, how she handled the shop problem, how many questions she needed in the guessing game, and whether the school or location topic should come next.
