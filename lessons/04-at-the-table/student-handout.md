# Lesson 4 — At the table · За столом

## Today I can ...

- offer food or drink using informal `ti` and polite `Vi`
- accept or decline naturally
- ask for something at the table
- express a simple preference or reaction

## Cumulative conversation

Greet someone, ask how they are, introduce one person, and say one true family fact. Add one follow-up question.

## Listen and read

**Baka:** Amaya, hoćeš li pitu?  
*Amaya, would you like some pie?*

**Amaya:** Da, molim. Hvala!  
*Yes, please. Thank you!*

**Baka:** A Marko, hoćeš li još?  
*And Marko, would you like some more?*

**Marko:** Ne, hvala. Sit sam.  
*No, thank you. I’m full.*

**Amaya:** Voda, molim.  
*Water, please.*

## Useful language

| Serbian (Latin) | English | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Hoćeš li ...? | Would you like ...? | informal singular |
| Hoćete li ...? | Would you like ...? | polite or plural |
| Da, molim. | Yes, please. | accepting |
| Ne, hvala. | No, thank you. | declining |
| još | more; another | `Još pite?` |
| Mogu li da dobijem ...? | May I have ...? | polite request |
| Izvolite. | Here you are; please. | polite or plural |
| Volim / Ne volim ... | I like / don’t like ... | true preference |
| Ukusno je. | It is delicious. | reaction |
| Sita sam. / Sit sam. | I am full. | female / male speaker |

## Food and drink

`pita` — pie · `hleb` — bread · `sir` — cheese · `salata` — salad · `voda` — water · `kafa` — coffee

Choose the foods that are useful for your real conversations. The others are recognition vocabulary today.

## Pattern in useful chunks

> Hoćeš li pitu? — Da, molim.  
> Hoćete li kafu? — Ne, hvala.  
> Mogu li da dobijem vodu? — Izvolite.

The food words may change form inside longer phrases (`pita` → `pitu`, `voda` → `vodu`). Learn the whole request; do not memorize a case table yet.

## Pronunciation: `š` and `ž`

- `š = ш`: `šta`, `još`
- `ž = ж`: `žena`, `kaže`

Say: `Hoćeš li još?` · `Kaže da je ukusno.` Keep `o` in `još` short and steady.

## Cyrillic bridge

Add `Ш = Š`, `Ж = Ž`, `З = Z`, `Г = G`, and `Ф = F`. Decode these familiar words before checking Latin:

| Cyrillic | Latin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| још | još | more |
| жена | žena | woman; wife |
| сир | sir | cheese |
| вода | voda | water |
| хлеб | hleb | bread |

## Reading — Sunday lunch

Amaya i Marko su kod bake i deke. Na stolu su pita, salata, sir i hleb. Baka pita: „Amaya, hoćeš li još pite?” Amaya kaže: „Da, molim. Pita je veoma ukusna!”

*Amaya and Marko are at Grandma and Grandpa’s home. Pie, salad, cheese, and bread are on the table. Grandma asks, “Amaya, would you like more pie?” Amaya says, “Yes, please. The pie is very delicious!”*

New support: `baka/deka` — grandmother/grandfather; `na stolu` — on the table; `veoma` — very.

### Talk about it

1. Where are Amaya and Marko?
2. What is on the table?
3. What does Grandma offer and how does Amaya answer?
4. What would be on the table at your ideal Serbian meal?
5. What food would you accept, decline, or ask about?

Retell the lunch in your own words, then ask your partner one follow-up question.

## Communication lab

Use real dishes, photos, or objects. First speak with Marko using `ti`. Then repeat with an older relative using `Vi`. Offer, accept or decline, request something across the table, and give a true reaction. Change roles.

## Optional repair prompts

Use only if a phrase breaks down:

- polite: `Hoćete / Hoćeš li kafu?`
- accept: `Da, ______.`
- request: `Mogu li da ______ vodu?`

Answers: `Hoćete`; `molim`; `dobijem`.
