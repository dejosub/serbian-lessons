# Lesson 11 teacher guide — Plans, months, and colors

## Target

Amaya can make a plan with a day and time, decline one option and agree on another, say when birthdays are, and understand and answer a short family message. She can then describe the colors of clothes, food, and places with correct masculine, feminine, and neuter endings in common phrases, and complete a polite clothes-shop exchange.

## Before the lesson

- Lesson 10 reached its grammar slide (slide 6 of 14) and stopped there; the accusative slide went very well. Everything after it that is about time and the calendar is the first half of this lesson: the Friday-night dialogue, the plan phrases, months and birthdays, family holidays, and Baka’s message. Colors are the second half.
- Make or bring the two “busy week” cards, one for each of you, each with four or five commitments on different days and times (for example `у понедељак ради`, `у среду увече бас`, `у суботу ујутру пијаца`), with only one or two free slots in common.
- Know the birthday months of three family members, and the family’s slava (saint and date).
- Decide how Baka would address Amaya in a message; replace `Драга Амаја` with `Драга Амајо` if that is what she would write.
- Gather eight to ten colored objects you can touch: clothes, fruit, a mug, a book, a pen. Find one autumn photo of Kalemegdan or the Danube in Belgrade.
- For the shop scenario, prepare one problem: the jacket is not available in her color, it is too expensive, or it comes only in dark blue.
- Amaya enjoyed the word roots of the days of the week. Read the Word origins slide beforehand and let her guess each origin before you give it.

## Sequence — 60 minutes

1. **Listen: Friday night — 4 min:** Listen with print hidden: what is the plan, and when? Then switch roles and change the event, day, and time.
2. **Make a plan — 6 min:** Read the six phrases once, then plan with the busy-week cards: a walk, a concert, lunch with Baka and Deda. Refuse her first suggestion (`Не могу у суботу, радим.`) and wait for a counterproposal.
3. **Months and birthdays — 5 min:** Read the months once together. `Када ти је рођендан? — У ...` for both of you, Marko, and one more person. Mention Нова година, Божић (7 January), and the family slava: `Када је ваша слава?` `у августу` stays a chunk here; it is the locative, which comes in Lesson 13.
4. **Read: Baka’s message — 5 min:** Latin help covers the long words. Read once for the event, once for the options.
5. **React and reply — 5 min:** The three questions, then call Baka: she replies with `Драга бако ...`. As Baka, change the time (`Не у један—у два!`) and add a request (`Донесите хлеб!`) to see whether she confirms.
6. **Colors — 4 min:** Touch objects and ask `Које је боје?`; she repeats, then asks you. Go through the swatches, then `светло-` and `тамно-`.
7. **Word origins — 4 min:** Before each arrow, ask her to guess: what could `црвен` have to do with `црв`? What does `-аст` add? Finish with `ружичаст`. If she asks for more, `зелен` is related to `зеље` (greens) and `жут` to `злато` (gold) in older Slavic; keep it to one or two extras.
8. **Grammar slide: gender and agreement — 12 min:** Name `род` and `слагање`, then work through the example bank one noun at a time: she gives each real object a color with the right ending (`-`, `-а`, `-о`). Finish with the accusative row: the jacket is what the wearing happens to, so it is the object, as `кафу` was in Lesson 10. Add the row to the grammar map. Mention `плави џемпер` once; do not drill definite forms.
9. **Clothes — 3 min:** `Шта носиш данас? — Носим ...` about both of you; notice `плаву јакну`.
10. **Read: Kalemegdan — 5 min:** Read once for the place and season, once for the colors.
11. **Discussion — 3 min:** Look at the photo together and decide what color the Danube is.
12. **Clothes shop — 3 min:** One problem from the seller; she asks, compares, decides.
13. **Exit — 1 min**

The calendar half comes first because it was never practiced in class. If time runs short, stop after the clothes slide: the Kalemegdan reading and the shop become homework, and Lesson 12 can open with them.

## Grammar example bank — gender and agreement

- masculine, no ending: `џемпер`, `сир`, `хлеб`, `пасуљ`, `парк`, `пас`, `чај`, `Дунав`
- feminine, `-а`: `јакна`, `кафа`, `вода`, `пита`, `сарма`, `јабука`, `мачка`, `кућа`
- neuter, `-о` or `-е`: `небо`, `место`, `дрвеће`
- accusative, only feminine changes (for things): `Носим плаву јакну.` `Имам белу мачку.` `Волим зелени чај.`

## Push moves

- In Make a plan, refuse her first suggestion and wait; then ask `Шта да донесемо?` back to her and let her decide.
- In the birthday round, answer with a different month than she expects and let her confirm: `У јуну? Не у јулу?`
- In the shop: `Немамо плаву, али имамо тамноплаву.` or `Ова кошта десет хиљада динара.` Wait for her next question.

## Boundaries

In the first half, correct only `у` + day (`у суботу`, not `у субота`) and the plan phrases; the vocative `бако` is for recognition and one useful phrase. In the second half, correct agreement in the phrases she uses most (`плава јакна`, `плаво небо`); let other slips go. `у августу` and `у плавој боји` are ready-made chunks, not a lesson on the locative.

## Script and pronunciation

Primarily Cyrillic; every new word and phrase has its Latin beside it on the slide, and long words in the readings are in Latin at the bottom. Model question rhythm on `Када?`, `У колико сати?`, and `Хоћеш ли да идемо?`. The vowel-less `р` (`цр-вен`, `Ср-би-ја`) is in the handout; point it out when `црвен` and `црн` come up.

## Session note for Lesson 12

Record how far you got and how long the grammar slide took, whether she proposed and declined plans without the slide, which months she produced without hesitation, how she handled Baka’s message, which colors she used independently, and how agreement held up in fast speech.
