# Lesson 10 homework — Домаћи задатак

Choose three activities, about ten minutes each.

1. **Read and reply:** Read Baka’s message in Cyrillic, using the Latin help. Record or write a reply: wish her a happy birthday, say which day you can come and when, and ask what to bring.
2. **My week voice note:** Describe your real week in 60–90 seconds: what you did yesterday, what you are doing tomorrow, and one plan for the weekend. Use at least four days.
3. **Calendar in Serbian:** For one week, say the day aloud every morning: `Данас је ...` Add one plan: `Увече ...`
4. **Conversation with Marko:** Plan something real for next week. Ask `Шта радиш у ...?`, propose a day and time, and make Marko say no at least once. Agree on a plan and finish with `Важи!`
5. **Family holidays:** Ask Marko `Када је ваша слава?` and `Шта радите за Божић?` Note three new facts and retell them in Serbian.

## Self-check

Translate: “What are you doing on Saturday? Do you want to go to the park? — I can’t, I’m sorry. How about Sunday?”

Answer: `Шта радиш у суботу? Хоћеш ли да идемо у парк? — Не могу, жао ми је. Може ли у недељу?` · `Šta radiš u subotu? Hoćeš li da idemo u park? — Ne mogu, žao mi je. Može li u nedelju?`

Translate: “My birthday is in August.”

Answer: `Рођендан ми је у августу.` · `Rođendan mi je u avgustu.`
