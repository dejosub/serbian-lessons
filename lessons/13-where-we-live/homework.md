# Lesson 13 homework — Домаћи задатак

Choose three activities, about ten minutes each.

1. **Read and retell:** Read “Where Baka and Deda live” in Cyrillic, using the Latin help. Retell it without looking: where they live, what is in the village, and where they are in August and in winter.
2. **The way to ... voice note:** Record 60–90 seconds describing the way from your home to a place nearby—a bakery, a pharmacy, a park, or a station. Use `право`, `лево`, `десно`, `близу` or `далеко`, and at least three locatives (`у ...`, `на ...`).
3. **Sign hunt:** Find five Serbian signs online or in photos of Belgrade—shops, stations, streets. Read each one aloud and write it in the other script.
4. **Word origins:** Find two Serbian place names or words that are compounds and explain them in English or Serbian. For example, `Нови Сад` = `нови` (new) + `сад` (orchard, plantation; from `садити`, to plant). Ask Marko for one more.
5. **Conversation with Marko:** Ask where three family members live and where they usually are on Saturday morning: `Где живи ...?`, `Где је ... у суботу ујутру?` Report back in Serbian: `Бака живи на селу. У суботу ујутру је на пијаци.`
6. **Ask the way with Marko:** Marko is a stranger in Belgrade. Stop him politely, ask where two places are, and ask whether they are far. Marko gives one wrong direction—ask again until it is clear.

## Self-check

Translate: “Excuse me, where is the pharmacy? Is it far?”

Answer: `Извините, где се налази апотека? Да ли је далеко?` · `Izvinite, gde se nalazi apoteka? Da li je daleko?`

Translate: “I live in an apartment downtown. Baka and Deda live in the village.”

Answer: `Живим у стану у центру. Бака и деда живе на селу.` · `Živim u stanu u centru. Baka i deda žive na selu.`

Translate: “Marko is at work, and Baka is at the market.”

Answer: `Марко је на послу, а бака је на пијаци.` · `Marko je na poslu, a baka je na pijaci.`
