# Initial curriculum

The first six lessons form the starter course. Lessons 7–12 expand it into a conversational foundation. The sequence may change when the progress log shows a stronger real-life need.

| Lesson | Communication outcome | Main language pattern | Pronunciation / script |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Greet someone and introduce herself | `ja sam`, `ti si`; simple questions | Five vowel sounds; `j`, `r`; Latin |
| 2 | Ask and answer how someone is | question intonation; `ti` and `Vi` | `č/ć`, `dž/đ` |
| 3 | Talk about close family | gender; `moj/moja`; `imam` | `lj`, `nj`; equal Latin/Cyrillic |
| 4 | Ask for and discuss food and drinks | useful accusative chunks | `š`, `ž`; rhythm |
| 5 | Give age, phone number, and quantities | numbers; `imam ... godina` | first Cyrillic reading |
| 6 | Describe a normal day | common present-tense patterns | connected speech |
| 7 | Say what she likes and dislikes | `volim`, `ne volim`, `sviđa mi se` | contrast and review |
| 8 | Say where people live and where things are | `u` + location chunks | Cyrillic signs |
| 9 | Make simple plans | time, days, `hoću/želim/možemo` | question rhythm |
| 10 | Talk briefly about yesterday | introductory past tense | unstressed helper forms |
| 11 | Participate at a family meal | offers, requests, politeness | natural reductions |
| 12 | Hold a five-minute family conversation | consolidation | both scripts; assessment |

## Completion evidence

A lesson is complete when the learner can perform its communication outcome with light prompting. Finishing a worksheet alone is not completion.

After Lessons 6 and 12, record a short conversation and review:

- What she can do without notes
- Which phrases require prompts
- Which sounds reduce intelligibility
- Which topics motivate her
- What should be recycled, slowed down, or accelerated
