# Material style guide

## Four-line presentation

For new expressions, use:

> **Како си?**  
> **Kako si?**  
> *How are you?*  
> Informal; use with one person you know well.

In Lessons 1–2, Latin may appear first. From Lesson 3 onward, show both scripts with equal weight.

## Conventions

- Prefer short, natural Serbian over word-for-word English translations.
- Label formal and informal language.
- Keep a lesson to roughly 10–15 new lexical items.
- Put optional enrichment in a clearly marked section.
- Introduce case forms as useful phrases before naming or tabulating the case.
- Use the dialect selected in `course-profile.md` consistently.
- Have the Serbian-speaking partner verify examples against the language the family actually uses.
- Mark stress only when it is helpful and has been verified by a native speaker or reliable dictionary.

## Every lesson contains

1. A measurable communication outcome
2. A five-item cumulative review
3. A short realistic dialogue
4. Vocabulary in context
5. One pronunciation target
6. One manageable language pattern
7. Controlled practice
8. Personal questions
9. Role-play or listening
10. Small homework assignment and answer key
