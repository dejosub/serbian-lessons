# Lesson 7 teacher guide — Likes, interests, and pets

## Target

Amaya can sustain a four-minute conversation about preferences across two topics, ask follow-up questions, and give at least one simple reason.

## Sequence — 60 minutes

1. Cumulative day conversation — 7 min
2. Preference hook with real photos — 5 min
3. Dialogue listening and role switch — 7 min
4. `волим / не волим / свиђа ми се` through true contrasts — 8 min
5. Reading in Cyrillic — 7 min
6. Comprehension, reaction, and retelling — 8 min
7. Reasons with `зато што` — 5 min
8. Preference map for family and pets — 6 min
9. Two-topic open conversation — 5 min
10. Exit performance — 2 min

Cyrillic leads familiar material; Latin supports new or difficult expressions. Correct meaning-bearing target forms and return quickly to conversation.

## Session note for Lesson 8

Record spontaneous preference questions, successful reasons, recurring pronunciation or form issues, Cyrillic reading independence, and the strongest topic for future conversation.
