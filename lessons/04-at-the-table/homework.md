# Lesson 4 homework — Домаћи задатак

Choose three short activities during the week; each should take about 10 minutes.

## Read and react

Read “Sunday lunch” aloud, retell it without translating sentence by sentence, and give two true reactions about the foods.

## Real meal narration

At one meal, silently name three items in Serbian. Say or record one request, one preference, and one polite refusal.

## Cyrillic

Read `још`, `жена`, `сир`, `вода`, and `хлеб` before checking Latin. Then find two older words you can now read in Cyrillic.

## Voice note

Record a 45–60 second dinner-table exchange. Include one offer with `ti`, one with `Vi`, an acceptance or refusal, a request, and a reaction. Keep going through small mistakes.

## Self-check

Can you perform these without notes?

1. Would you like coffee? (polite)
2. No, thank you. I am full. (female speaker)
3. May I have some water?
4. It is delicious.

Answers: `Hoćete li kafu?`; `Ne, hvala. Sita sam.`; `Mogu li da dobijem vodu?`; `Ukusno je.`
