Lesson 1 · Lekcija 1

Serbian greetings
and introductions

Pozdravi i upoznavanje

Today: greet someone, exchange names, and say you are learning Serbian.
Listen first · Prvo slušaj

What words can you hear?

Ana: Zdravo! Ja sam Ana. Kako se zoveš?
Emi: Zdravo! Ja sam Emi. Drago mi je.
Listen once without reading. Recognizing even one word is a good start.
Dialogue · Dijalog

The whole conversation

Ana: Zdravo! Ja sam Ana. Kako se zoveš?Hi! I’m Ana. What’s your name?
Emi: Zdravo! Ja sam Emi. Drago mi je.Hi! I’m Emmy. Nice to meet you.
Ana: Drago mi je. Učiš srpski?Nice to meet you. Are you learning Serbian?
Emi: Da, učim srpski. Malo!Yes, I’m learning Serbian. A little!

Useful phrase · Koristan izraz

Nice to meet you

Drago mi je.

Learn it as one useful phrase. We will explain its grammar later.

Pronunciation · Izgovor

Five steady vowel sounds

aeiou
Keep each vowel short and pure: Ana · Emi · dobro · učim

Listen → pause → repeat. Avoid turning one vowel into two sounds.

Pronunciation · Izgovor

Two special sounds today

j = English y

ja · je
Drago mi je

r = tap or roll

An approximation is fine today. Keep speaking.

Role-play · Igra uloga

A family gathering

  1. Greet one person.
  2. Exchange names.
  3. Say: Učim srpski. Malo!
  4. Say: Vidimo se!
Reading · Čitanje

At the door

Ana je kod kuće. Emi dolazi.
Ana kaže: „Zdravo! Ja sam Ana.”
Emi kaže: „Ja sam Emi. Drago mi je.”
Emi uči srpski — malo!

kod kuće = at home · dolazi = arrives · kaže = says

Discussion · Razgovor

Talk about the reading

  • Where are Ana and Emi?
  • Is this their first meeting? What tells you?
  • What is Emi learning?
  • What would you say when you arrived?
English or mixed Serbian/English is welcome. Then retell what happens.
First Cyrillic letters

Five nearly free letters

А = AЕ = EО = OЈ = JМ = M
Ја сам

You already know Ja sam. Find Ј, А, С, and М; С is a supported extra today.

Exit check · Završna provera

Try it without notes

  • Say hello.
  • Introduce yourself.
  • Ask my name.
  • Say you are learning Serbian.
  • Say goodbye.
Bravo! You have completed your first Serbian conversation.
