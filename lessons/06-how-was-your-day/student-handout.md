# Lesson 6 — Како си провела дан? · How did you spend your day?

## Today I can ...

- ask where someone was and what they did
- answer with several ordinary completed actions
- connect events with `онда` and `после тога`
- read a familiar account in both scripts

## Conversation review

Greet your partner, describe one family member or pet, and ask one question about yesterday.

## Listen and read

**Марко:** Како си провела дан? · `Kako si provela dan?`  
*How did you spend your day?*

**Амаја:** Добро. Радила сам, ишла сам у продавницу и онда сам била код куће.  
`Dobro. Radila sam, išla sam u prodavnicu i onda sam bila kod kuće.`  
*Good. I worked, went to the store, and then I was at home.*

**Марко:** Шта си радила код куће? · `Šta si radila kod kuće?`  
**Амаја:** Јела сам и свирала бас. · `Jela sam i svirala bas.`

## Useful questions

| Cyrillic · Latin | English |
|---|---|
| Како си провела дан? · Kako si provela dan? | How did you spend your day? |
| Где си била? · Gde si bila? | Where were you? |
| Шта си радила? · Šta si radila? | What did you do? |
| С ким си била? · S kim si bila? | Who were you with? |

## Completed-action chunks

`била сам · bila sam` — I was · `ишла сам · išla sam` — I went · `радила сам · radila sam` — I worked · `јела сам · jela sam` — I ate · `видела сам · videla sam` — I saw · `свирала сам · svirala sam` — I played an instrument

These are female-speaker chunks. Learn and use them before analyzing the complete past-tense system.

## Connect the day

`прво · prvo` — first · `онда · onda` — then · `после тога · posle toga` — after that · `на крају · na kraju` — finally

## Reading — An ordinary Tuesday

Read in Latin for meaning:

> Ujutru je Amaya radila kod kuće. Posle toga je išla u prodavnicu. Videla je prijateljicu i kratko su razgovarale. Uveče je jela sa Markom, a onda je svirala bas. Dan nije bio poseban, ali je bio dobar.

Support: `ujutru/uveče` — in the morning/evening; `kratko` — briefly; `razgovarale su` — they talked; `poseban` — special.

Read again in Cyrillic:

> Ујутру је Амаја радила код куће. После тога је ишла у продавницу. Видела је пријатељицу и кратко су разговарале. Увече је јела са Марком, а онда је свирала бас. Дан није био посебан, али је био добар.

### Talk about it

1. Where was Amaya during the day?
2. Who did she see?
3. What did she do in the evening?
4. Why can an ordinary day still be good?
5. Which details are true of your day?

Retell from four prompts: `код куће` → `продавница` → `пријатељица` → `Марко и бас`.

## Conversation lab

Interview each other about today or yesterday. Ask at least three follow-up questions. Include one mundane detail—laundry, food, work, shopping, walking, music, or a phone call. English is available for an untaught word, but keep the question and action frame in Serbian.

## Optional repair

Put only these four events in a natural order: `radila sam`, `išla sam`, `jela sam`, `svirala sam`. Add connectors orally; do not turn this into a worksheet.
