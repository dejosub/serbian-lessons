# Lesson 9 homework — Домаћи задатак

Choose three activities, about ten minutes each.

1. **Read and retell:** Read “Morning at the market” in Cyrillic, using the Latin help. Retell it with all its numbers, then add one number of your own.
2. **Numbers around you:** For one day, say every price, time, or house number you see in Serbian under your breath. Write down the three hardest.
3. **Family numbers voice note:** Record 60–90 seconds: how old you are (or choose not to say!), how many brothers and sisters you have, and the age of one family member or pet.
4. **Conversation with Marko:** Ask `Колико имаш браће и сестара?` and `Колико година има твоја бака?` Then ask him to dictate three prices and his phone number fast. Write the digits, read them back in groups, and use `Можеш ли да поновиш?` at least once.
5. **Market role-play with Marko:** Marko is the seller. Buy three things for less than `1.000 динара`. He should make one mistake with the total; catch it.
