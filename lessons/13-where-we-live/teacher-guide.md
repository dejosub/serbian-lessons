# Lesson 13 teacher guide — Where we live

## Target

Amaya can say where she and family members live, name places in town and say whether they are near or far, stop someone politely and ask the way, read familiar signs in both scripts, and say where people are right now. She uses the locative with `у` and `на` in common places (`у Београду`, `на пијаци`, `на селу`, `у августу`).

## Before the lesson

- Read the Lesson 11 and Lesson 12 session notes; adjust the warm-up to whatever was not reached in Lesson 12.
- Months and birthdays moved to Lesson 11 with the rest of the unfinished Lesson 10 time material; here `у августу` is review that shows the same locative ending.
- Draw a simple street map (five or six streets, with пошта, пекара, апотека, библиотека, станица, пијаца) or open a phone map of central Belgrade for Ask the way.
- Adapt the Where is everyone? cues to real people and places if you like; for My street, have paper and a pen ready for the map.
- Confirm with the family where Baka and Deda really live and adapt the reading if you like (village or town; the yard and the apple tree).

## Sequence — 60 minutes

1. **Warm-up: school talk — 5 min:** The three questions on the slide; push with `А шта још?` Listen for present-tense endings from Lesson 12.
2. **Where do you live? — 4 min:** `Где живиш?` both ways (ти and Ви). Model `Живим у ...` with her real city and street; then Baka and Deda `на селу`.
3. **In town — 4 min:** Places, then `Где се налази ...?` with `близу / далеко` about real places near her home.
4. **Read the signs — 4 min:** She reads each sign aloud and says it in the other script. Mention that both scripts appear on real streets; `УЛАЗ / IZLAZ` are recognition only.
5. **Where words come from — 5 min:** Let her guess each origin before you explain. Start with `Београд` (бео + град); then the place suffix `-ница` (`продавница`, and `учионица` from Lesson 12), `пекара`, the Greek *thēkē* pair, and `стан / станица` from `стати`. Invite her own comparisons with languages she knows; keep it a conversation, not a list to learn.
6. **Grammar slide: the locative — 12 min:** Start from its role: the locative is the place where someone or something is or where something happens, and it only comes after a small word such as `у` or `на`. Point out what she already says: `у Београду` (Lesson 5), `на столу` (Lesson 4), `у парку` (Lesson 10). Then work through the example bank one noun at a time: she makes a true sentence with `Живим у ...`, `Бака је у ...`, `Марко је на ...`, or `... је на столу`. Finish with `у августу`—months from Lesson 11 use the same ending—and the `Идем у школу / У школи сам` contrast if she asks. Add the row to the grammar map.
7. **Where is everyone? — 4 min:** One locative sentence per cue (`Бака је на пијаци.`), then she asks you `Где си ти у суботу у десет?` and you answer. Point out `у суботу` (when, accusative) next to `на послу` (where, locative).
8. **Dialogue — 4 min:** Listen with print hidden: where is the post office, where is the bakery? Then switch roles with another place.
9. **Reading — 6 min:** Read once for where Baka and Deda live, once for what happens in August and in winter. Let her find the locatives (`на селу`, `у дворишту`, `у парку`, `у августу`, `у стану`, `у центру`).
10. **Discussion — 4 min**
11. **Scenarios — 6 min:** Ask the way on the map; My street if time remains, otherwise as homework with Marko.
12. **Exit — 2 min**

## Grammar example bank — locative

- feminine `-а → -и`: `кућа → у кући`, `школа → у школи`, `пијаца → на пијаци`, `пошта → у пошти / на пошти`, `пекара → у пекари`, `апотека → у апотеци`, `продавница → у продавници`, `улица → у улици`, `зграда → у згради`, `Србија → у Србији`
- masculine `+ у`: `град → у граду`, `Београд → у Београду`, `парк → у парку`, `стан → у стану`, `центар → у центру` (loses its `а`), `сто → на столу`, `концерт → на концерту`, `посао → на послу`
- neuter `-о → -у`: `село → на селу`, `место → на месту`
- months (review from Lesson 11): `август → у августу`, `мај → у мају`, `децембар → у децембру`
- watch `к → ц`: `апотека → у апотеци`, `река → на реци`, `Америка → у Америци`

## Push moves

- In Ask the way, send her the wrong way once (`Лево—не, извините, десно!`), say one place is closed (`Пошта ради до пет.`), or say it is far and wait for her next question.
- When she says where someone lives, ask `А где је то?` or `Да ли је далеко?`
- In My street, draw one place in the wrong spot and wait for her to correct you: `Не, пекара је десно!`

## Boundaries

The locative singular is the grammar focus: name it by its role, then the two endings (`-и` for feminine, `-у` for masculine and neuter). `у` vs `на` is learned by phrase, not by rule; accept `у пијаци` once, then recast as `на пијаци`. Plurals, adjectives in the locative (`у старој згради`), and the full `у + accusative` contrast stay recognition only. `поред поште` is a genitive chunk. Do not drill `Где би волела да живиш?`—it is a discussion question with its gloss.

## Script and pronunciation

Primarily Cyrillic; every new place word and phrase has its Latin beside it on the slide, and long words in the reading are in Latin at the bottom. The script focus is real signs: she reads Latin and Cyrillic signs alike and switches between them. Model `Извините, ...` with a polite falling tone and the question rise on the place: `где се налази пошта?`
