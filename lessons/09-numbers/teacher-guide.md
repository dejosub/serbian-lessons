# Lesson 9 teacher guide — Numbers in everyday life

## Target

In a market role-play, Amaya can ask at least three prices, buy two items in specific quantities, and confirm the total. In conversation, she can give and ask ages, family numbers, and a phone number, and ask for repetition when needed.

## Before the lesson

- Write five prices and two phone numbers for dictation (for example `120 динара`, `350`, `1.200`, `064 123 45 67`, `063 80 90 15`). Keep them hidden.
- Prepare play money or slips of paper adding up to `1.000 динара` for the market stall.
- Choose one real family photo for the photo-album scenario.
- Read the Lesson 8 session note and pick two feelings to recycle.

## Sequence — 60 minutes

1. **Cumulative conversation — 6 min:** Feelings and weekend preferences; push with `Зашто?` and `А шта још?`
2. **Numbers 0–10 — 5 min:** Count up, down, and by twos. Point at fingers or objects at random; speed matters more than spelling.
3. **11–100 patterns — 6 min:** Show `-наест` and `-десет`, then play number ping-pong: you say a number, Amaya says the next one; switch to tens. Add `сто`, `двеста`, `триста`, `хиљада`.
4. **Listening — 5 min:** Dictate your hidden prices and phone numbers; Amaya writes digits only. Then she dictates three to you. Read phone numbers in groups.
5. **Hard ч, soft ћ — 3 min:** Mix `четири, четрнаест, четрдесет, продавачица` with `ћерка, хоћу, кућа, срећна`; Amaya points. Finish with `четири ћерке`, then `Ђак има четири наранџе у џепу` for all four sounds.
6. **Age and family numbers — 5 min:** Ask and answer `Колико имаш година?` and `Колико имаш браће и сестара?` about real people. Use `Ви` once.
7. **Dialogue at the market — 5 min:** Listen with print hidden for the price and items; switch roles.
8. **Numbers change the noun — 2 min:** Point out `два динара / две јабуке / пет јабука`. Name it as a pattern, not a rule to drill.
9. **Reading in Cyrillic — 6 min:** Latin help covers the long words. Read once for the story, once for the numbers.
10. **Discussion and retelling — 6 min:** Include `Лепо говорите српски! — Хвала, још учим.`
11. **Scenarios — 9 min:** Market stall and one of the other two.
12. **Exit — 2 min**

## Push moves

At the market stall, add complications:

- give a price that seems too high and wait for `Скупо!` or a question
- say the total wrong and see whether she checks it with `Колико је то?`
- offer more: `Још нешто? Можда пита?`
- speak a phone number fast so she must ask `Можете ли да поновите?`

## Boundaries

Numbers are the new language; Latin help stays on every slide that introduces them. Accept `два/две` or noun-ending slips if the number is clear—correct them once in a recast and move on. Teach `година/године/годину` only through the ages that come up.

## Script and pronunciation

Primarily Cyrillic, with Latin for numbers and long words. Model number stress by repeating natural groups (`шездесет четири`, `сто педесет`) rather than single digits. `четрдесет` previews the vowel-less `р` that appears again in Lesson 11.

## Session note for Lesson 10

Record which numbers she still needs to count up to, how well she understood numbers at natural speed, whether she asked for repetition, how independent the market purchase was, and which scenario generated the most conversation.
