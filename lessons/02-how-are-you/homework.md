# Lesson 2 homework

Practice for about 10 minutes on four days.

## Day 1 — Listen and answer

Read “A quick call” aloud, explain what happens without translating line by line, and answer one discussion question. Then play the teacher’s question recording and give a true answer.

## Day 2 — Choose the relationship

Write `ti` or `Vi`: close friend; two family members; unfamiliar cashier; child; older adult you have just met. Discuss family-specific choices with your teacher.

## Day 3 — Sound practice

Listen and repeat three times: `čaj — ćao`; `džem — Đorđe`; `odlično — kuća — gospođa`. Accuracy can improve over time.

Then read these already-known phrases in Cyrillic: `Како си?`, `А ти?`, `Хвала.` Check the Latin versions in the handout after your first attempt.

## Day 4 — Voice note

Record two miniature conversations: one with a friend using `ti`, and one polite exchange using `Vi`.

## Written check

1. Kako ___? — Dobro sam, hvala. A ti?  
2. Kako ___? — Nije loše. A Vi?  
3. Translate: “I’m great.”  
4. Translate for a female speaker: “I’m tired.”

## Answer key

1. `si`  
2. `ste`  
3. `Odlično sam.`  
4. `Umorna sam.`
