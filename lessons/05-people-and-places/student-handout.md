# Lesson 5 — People and places · Људи и места

## Today I can ...

- describe a person with two details
- say where someone is
- describe a place and connect ideas
- read a short familiar passage in Cyrillic with controlled support

## Cumulative conversation

Greet someone, introduce one real person, then offer and request something at the table. Continue naturally with one follow-up question.

## Listen and read

**Amaya:** Ko je ovo?  
*Who is this?*

**Marko:** Ovo je moja baka. Ona je veoma topla i duhovita.  
*This is my grandmother. She is very warm and funny.*

**Amaya:** A gde je ona?  
*And where is she?*

**Marko:** Ona je u Beogradu. Beograd je veliki i živ.  
*She is in Belgrade. Belgrade is big and lively.*

## Useful language

| Serbian | English |
|---|---|
| Ko je ovo? | Who is this? |
| On je ... / Ona je ... | He is ... / She is ... |
| Gde je ...? | Where is ...? |
| On/Ona je u ... | He/She is in ... |
| ovde / tamo | here / there |
| i / ali | and / but |
| veoma | very |

## Describing people and places

| masculine phrase | feminine phrase | English |
|---|---|---|
| visok | visoka | tall |
| mlad | mlada | young |
| topao | topla | warm |
| duhovit | duhovita | funny |
| miran | mirna | calm |
| velik | velika | big |
| mali | mala | small |
| lep | lepa | beautiful; nice |
| živ | živa | lively |

Select four descriptions that fit people or places you genuinely want to discuss. The rest are recognition items.

## Pattern to notice

Descriptions often change with the word they describe: `Marko je visok`; `Ana je visoka`; `grad je velik`; `kuća je velika`. Use meaningful pairs before trying to state a rule. Connect details with `i`, `ali`, and `veoma`.

## Pronunciation and Cyrillic

Recycle the contrasts `č/ć` and `dž/đ` in already-known words before adding their letters:

`Ч = Č` · `Ћ = Ć` · `Џ = DŽ` · `Ђ = Đ` · `У = U`

Decode: `кућа` (`kuća`), `дечко` (`dečko`), `Ђорђе` (`Đorđe`), `џем` (`džem`). Say the familiar word first, then connect sound and letter.

## Controlled Cyrillic reading

Read each familiar sentence in Cyrillic before checking Latin:

> Она је у Београду.  
> *Ona je u Beogradu.*

> Град је велики и жив.  
> *Grad je veliki i živ.*

Read the whole phrase once, identify only the blocking letter, and try again.

## Reading — At Kalemegdan

Mila i njen deda su na Kalemegdanu. Deda je miran i duhovit. Park je velik i lep. Beograd je danas veoma živ. Mila voli ovo mesto.

*Mila and her grandfather are at Kalemegdan. Her grandfather is calm and funny. The park is big and beautiful. Belgrade is very lively today. Mila loves this place.*

Support: `deda` — grandfather; `miran` — calm; `park` — park; `mesto` — place.

### Talk about it

1. Who is with Mila and where are they?
2. How is her grandfather described?
3. What is the park like? What is Belgrade like?
4. What person and place would you connect in your own story?
5. What makes that place important to you?

Retell the passage, then tell your own photo story and ask a follow-up question.

## Communication lab

Choose two photos: one person and one place. For each, give at least four connected sentences using `i`, `ali`, or `veoma`. Your partner asks questions; then switch roles. Include one description you disagree about and negotiate it naturally.

## Optional repair prompts

Use only if conversation reveals a gap:

- Ana je visok / visoka.
- Grad je velik, ___ lep.
- ___ je Marko? — U Beogradu.

Answers: `visoka`; `ali` or `i`; `Gde`.
