# Lesson 3 teacher guide — My family

## Target

The learner can identify and introduce at least three real people using `Ovo je moj/moja ...` and state one true family fact with `Imam` or `Nemam`.

## Before the lesson

- Ask the learner to bring a few family photos or use a hand-drawn family map.
- Confirm the words the family actually uses (`mama/majka`, `tata/otac`, partner labels).
- Record the dialogue naturally and slowly in the selected dialect.
- Show Cyrillic and Latin equally; do not require Cyrillic writing yet.

## Suggested sequence (45–60 minutes)

1. **Cumulative review — 7 min:** Full greeting and `Kako si/ste?` exchange.
2. **Personal hook — 5 min:** Show one photo and say `Ovo je moja/ moj ...`; let meaning emerge.
3. **Dialogue — 10 min:** Listen, sequence four dialogue lines, then read in Latin. Do not show or require the Cyrillic dialogue before the letter bridge.
4. **Vocabulary — 8 min:** Select only the 6–8 family words relevant to the learner; remaining words are recognition items.
5. **`moj/moja` pattern — 8 min:** Sort the selected words into two groups. Avoid a complete gender or case lecture.
6. **`lj/nj` — 5 min:** Model names and short words in Latin, then connect the already-heard sounds to Cyrillic `љ/њ`.
7. **Cyrillic bridge — 8 min:** Introduce `Б Р Н П Д Ц Љ Њ`, then decode only familiar words: `брат`, `син`, `породица`, `љубав`, `њен`. Read Cyrillic first and check against Latin. Do not assess the full dialogue in Cyrillic.
8. **Selective practice — 5 min:** Use A and B for patterns that still need support; skip repetitive items after the learner demonstrates reliable choices.
9. **Reading and discussion — 10 min:** Read “A photo” in Latin for meaning, establish the people and location, then invite a real family-photo story. After discussion, offer the Cyrillic version as a supported second pass; it contains preview letters and is not an independent decoding test. Ask for a short retelling.
10. **Photo role-play — 8 min:** Ask `Ko je ovo?`; learner introduces real people. Reverse roles.
11. **Exit check — 4 min:** Three people plus one `Imam/Nemam` sentence without notes.

## Important grammar note

The dialogue contains `jednog brata` and `jednu sestru`. Treat these as whole useful chunks. Do not teach the accusative paradigm yet. If the number is not needed, `Imam brata/sestru` is enough.

## Answer key

- Practice A: `moj brat`, `moja mama`, `moj dečko`, `moja sestra`, `moj tata`, `moja devojka`.
- Practice B: `Ko je ovo? — Ovo je moj brat, Luka. Imam jednu sestru. Ovo je moja sestra, Ana.`

## Observe for Lesson 4

- Which family words are genuinely useful and should recur?
- Does the learner select `moj/moja` by noun reliably?
- Can she recognize the target letters and decode the five familiar target words with Latin available for checking?
- Does `lj` or `nj` affect intelligibility?

## Cyrillic boundary

English leads instructions and Latin leads all new language in Lesson 3. The learner is responsible only for the explicit target set and five familiar decoding words. The full Cyrillic reading is supported exposure after comprehension, not evidence that she should already read Cyrillic independently.
