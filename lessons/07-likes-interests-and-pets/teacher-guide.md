# Lesson 7 teacher guide — Likes, interests, and pets

## Target

Amaya can sustain a four-minute conversation about preferences across two topics, ask follow-up questions, and give at least one simple reason. She can hear and produce the hard `ч/џ` versus soft `ћ/ђ` contrast in familiar words.

## Sequence — 60 minutes

1. **Cumulative day conversation — 6 min:** Push after each answer with one more question.
2. **Two sound families — 8 min:** Present hard `ч/џ` and soft `ћ/ђ` as two pairs. Round 1: say `мачка, кућа, чита, хоћу, џез, свиђа, џемпер, госпођа` in random order with print hidden; Amaya points. Round 2: the listening-only pairs `чар/ћар` and `џак/ђак`. Round 3: Amaya says four words and you point—tell her honestly which ones you heard as hard or soft. Do not exaggerate articulation; contrast only the missed sound.
3. **Dialogue listening and role switch — 5 min**
4. **Preference scale — 7 min:** Place `обожавам · волим · више волим ... него ... · не волим · уопште не волим` on a scale through true contrasts (bass, coffee, tea, pita, pets).
5. **Questions — 4 min:** `Да ли волиш ...?`, `А шта још?`, and `Зашто?` Amaya asks; answer briefly so she must ask again.
6. **Reading in Cyrillic — 6 min:** Use the Latin help at the bottom of the slide for long words; do not make her decode `породицом` or `вечерају` letter by letter.
7. **Comprehension, reaction, and retelling — 7 min**
8. **Scenarios — 9 min:** Choose two of the three and play the other role yourself.
9. **Preference map and `слика љубимца` — 4 min:** When a pet photo appears, compare `љубимац → љубимца` and name the genitive and disappearing `а` briefly.
10. **Open conversation and exit — 4 min**

Cyrillic leads familiar material; Latin supports new or difficult expressions. Correct meaning-bearing target forms and return quickly to conversation.

## Push moves

In-person pushing works well. In every scenario, add one complication and wait for Amaya to ask the next question:

- give a short or surprising answer (`Не волим музику.`) so she must ask `Зашто?`
- answer only half the question so she must ask `А шта још?`
- as Baka, offer more food after she says she is full
- as the friend, say the dog is not calm at all

Model stress in whole words and short phrases; do not test accent labels.

## Session note for Lesson 8

Record spontaneous preference questions, successful reasons, how well she heard and produced hard vs. soft, Cyrillic reading independence with Latin help, and the strongest topic for future conversation.
