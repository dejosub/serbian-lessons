# Lesson 6 homework — Домаћи задатак

Choose three ten-minute activities.

1. **Read and retell:** Read “An ordinary Tuesday” in Latin, retell from four prompts, then read it in Cyrillic.
2. **Three-day mini-log:** On three days, record three Serbian chunks about what you did; fragments are acceptable.
3. **Voice note:** Record 60–90 seconds answering `Где си била?`, `Шта си радила?`, and `С ким си била?` Use two connectors.
4. **Real interview with Marko:** Ask `Kako si proveo dan?`, then use `Gde si bio?`, `Šta si radio?`, and `S kim si bio?` as follow-ups. Ask one unscripted `A posle toga?` or `Zašto?`. Retell at least three of his actions to him in English or mixed Serbian, and let him correct the sequence.

Self-check: “I worked, then I went to the store, and finally I was at home.” → `Радила сам, онда сам ишла у продавницу и на крају сам била код куће.`
