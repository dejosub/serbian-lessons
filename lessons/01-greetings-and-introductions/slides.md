# Serbian 1
## Greetings and introductions

Today: greet someone, exchange names, and say you are learning Serbian.

---

# Listen first

What words can you hear?

> Zdravo! Ja sam Ana. Kako se zoveš?  
> Zdravo! Ja sam Emi. Drago mi je.

---

# The whole dialogue

**Ana:** Zdravo! Ja sam Ana. Kako se zoveš?  
**Emi:** Zdravo! Ja sam Emi. Drago mi je.  
**Ana:** Drago mi je. Učiš srpski?  
**Emi:** Da, učim srpski. Malo!

---

# Three essential moves

**Zdravo!** — Hi!  
**Ja sam ...** — I am ...  
**Kako se zoveš?** — What is your name?

Здраво! · Ја сам ... · Како се зовеш?

---

# Nice to meet you

## Drago mi je.

Драго ми је.

Say it as one useful phrase. We will explain its grammar later.

---

# Five vowel sounds

`a` · `e` · `i` · `o` · `u`

Keep each vowel short and steady.

Ana · Emi · dobro · učim

---

# Two special sounds today

`j` = English **y** in *yes*

`ja` · `je` · `Drago mi je`

`r` is tapped or rolled. An approximation is fine today.

---

# A small pattern

| Serbian | English |
|---|---|
| ja sam | I am |
| ti si | you are |

Full verb table? Not today. Use the phrases.

# One real-life exchange

You have just arrived at a family gathering.

1. Greet one person.
2. Exchange names.
3. Say: **Učim srpski. Malo!**
4. Say: **Vidimo se!**
