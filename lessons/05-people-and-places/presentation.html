Who does what? A small case map

Nominative = who is there or being identified
Ovo je Ana. · Ana je visoka.

Accusative = the direct object after an action
Vidim Anu. · Opisujem Anu.

What changed? For many feminine nouns: -a → -u
Ana → Anu · sestra → sestru · voda → vodu

Cyrillic reading strategy

Read a whole familiar phrase. Mark only the blocking letter. Check Latin after a full attempt.

Она је у Београду.
Град је велики и жив.

The final five Cyrillic letters

Ч = Č · Ћ = Ć · Џ = DŽ · Ђ = Đ · У = U

кућа · дечко · Ђорђе · џем

The sounds and words are familiar; these symbols are new. All 30 letters have now been introduced.

Read a poem in Latin

Ala je lep ovaj svet,
onde potok, ovde cvet;
tamo njiva, ovde sad,
eno sunce, evo hlad.
Tamo Dunav, zlata pun,
onde trava, ovde žbun.
Slavuj peva, ne znam gdi —
ovde srce, ovde ti!

Key words: potok — stream · cvet — flower · njiva — field · hlad — shade
žbun — bush · slavuj — nightingale · gdi — old poetic gde

Discuss and react

  • What can the speaker see and hear?
  • Which words mean here and there?
  • What mood does the poem create?
  • What would you change to describe today?

From the poem to your world

What would your version of ovaj svet include?

  1. Choose one meaningful person or pet in a real place.
  2. Say who and where.
  3. Give two descriptions.
  4. Explain why they belong in your world.

Ask one follow-up question.
