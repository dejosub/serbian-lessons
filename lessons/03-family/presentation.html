Pronunciation

lj and nj

lj

ljubav · Ljiljana

nj

njen · Njegoš

Each is one joined sound. In Cyrillic, each also has one letter: Љ = LJ · Њ = NJ.

My family · Moja porodica

Introduce three people

Ovo je moj / moja ______.

Then add one true sentence with Imam or Nemam.

Reading

An old photo

Emi gleda staru fotografiju.
Ovo je njena mama, a ovo je njen brat Luka.
Luka je sada u Beogradu.

gleda = looks at · stara = old · sada = now

Read for meaning in Latin. The handout offers a supported Cyrillic second pass.
Discussion

Talk about the reading

  • What is Emi looking at?
  • Who is in the photo?
  • Where is Luka now?
  • What old family photo would you choose?
Retell Emi’s story, then connect it to a real family story.
Exit check

Try without notes

  • Ask who someone is.
  • Introduce three people.
  • Say one true family fact.
Bravo! You can introduce your family.
