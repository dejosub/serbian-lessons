# Lesson 3 homework — Домаћи задатак

Practice for about 10 minutes on four days.

## Day 1 — Family labels

Label three real or fictional people in Latin, for example: `moja mama`. If the word uses only introduced Cyrillic letters, copy its Cyrillic form from the handout as optional practice.

## Day 2 — Cyrillic decoding

Read the five familiar targets: `брат`, `син`, `породица`, `љубав`, `њен`. Check each against Latin. Then read the expanded “A photo” in Latin, retell it from the prompts `fotografija` → `mama, Luka, baka` → `Beograd / Niš`, and answer one discussion question. Read the Cyrillic version as a supported second pass, not a speed test.

## Day 3 — `lj/nj` and `љ/њ`

Listen and repeat: `љубав · ljubav`, `Љиљана · Ljiljana`, `њен · njen`, `Његош · Njegoš`.

## Day 4 — Voice note

Using a photo, record 45–60 seconds. Introduce three people, say where one person is, and add one true sentence with `Имам / Imam` or `Немам / Nemam`.

## Written check

1. ___ брат (`moj` or `moja`)  
2. ___ сестра (`moj` or `moja`)  
3. Translate: “This is my mom.”  
4. Translate: “Who is this?”  
5. Read: `породица`, `брат`, `син`.

## Answer key

1. `moj brat`  
2. `moja sestra`  
3. `Ovo je moja mama. · Ово је моја мама.`  
4. `Ko je ovo? · Ко је ово?`  
5. `porodica` — family; `brat` — brother; `sin` — son.
